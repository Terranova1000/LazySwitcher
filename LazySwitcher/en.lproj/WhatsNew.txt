• A word is now corrected when punctuation follows it: «ьфкиду?», «ьфкиду!»,
  «ьфкиду.», «ьфкиду,» — previously only a space worked.

  The mark itself is left exactly as typed: whoever typed a question mark
  meant one, and the same key gives "&" in the other layout.

  The subtlety that made this slower than it looks: the "." and "," keys type
  "ю" and "б" on a Cyrillic layout. Treating the key as a separator would cut
  Russian words in half. So the application looks at the character that
  appeared, and works it out afresh whenever the layout changes.

  The hyphen is deliberately excluded: «почему-то» is one word.

• Fixed the crash at launch on macOS 26 (from 1.12.3).

• Development builds no longer offer to update themselves — that used to
  produce "the signature does not match" about nothing.
