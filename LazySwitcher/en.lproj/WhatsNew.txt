• Fixed a bug where, after a while — most often after the Mac had slept —
  the app could start damaging correctly typed text: letters were doubled
  and ordinary Russian words turned into Latin nonsense.

• Added a safeguard for the future: if a word does not look like any language
  after replacement, the app leaves it alone. Whatever happens, nonsense will
  no longer take the place of a normal word.

• Words containing «б», «ю» and «ж» typed on the English layout are corrected
  whole again. Version 1.13 cut them at those letters. A full stop, a comma
  and other marks after a word still work.
