Fixing what I broke in yesterday's 1.12.

• Selections work again — and now they really do.

  Conversion always went "out of the currently active layout". But what you
  select is precisely the text typed in the *other* one, and by the time you
  notice, the keyboard has usually been switched back. So the English layout
  was being asked to explain Russian letters, recognised nothing, and the
  whole phrase was refused with a beep.

  The direction is now read from the text itself. Select anything, press
  double Shift, and it converts either way.

• The hotkey no longer needs a second press. The selection check had jumped
  ahead of finding out what the cursor is in, so the first press landed on a
  field we did not know yet.

From 1.12, in case you missed it:

• Corrections were being applied and recorded as failures — so the keyboard
  layout was never switched. You fixed a word, carried on typing in the same
  wrong layout, and the next correction came out wrong.

• A word is no longer thrown away when the application has not yet said what
  the cursor is in. It waits for the answer.
