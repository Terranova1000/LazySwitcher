• Fixed: the automatic correction sometimes did nothing for the first word,
  and you had to erase it and type it again.

  The erasing was not what helped. The application asks the system what kind
  of field the cursor is in, and the answer sometimes arrived later than you
  could type that first word. Until it arrives, touching the text is not
  allowed — it might be a password. Erasing and retyping takes a few seconds,
  and the answer turned up in between.

  It now asks about the field right after a click, and once more at the end
  of a word if the answer is still missing.

• The application no longer falls asleep with your Mac. After waking or
  restarting it would sometimes do nothing until you woke it with a
  double Shift.

• Fixed the problems that showed up on macOS 26: the menu bar icon going
  missing, line breaks being swallowed, and the application occasionally
  switching itself off without saying so.

• The space between two corrections in a row no longer disappears.
