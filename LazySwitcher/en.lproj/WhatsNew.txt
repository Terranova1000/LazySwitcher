• Fixed a crash at launch on macOS 26. The application could die at the
  moment it connects to the keyboard — silently, with no error window. Hence
  "neither the hotkey nor the automatic correction works".

• A correction no longer tries to shorten itself blindly: when there is less
  text before the cursor than needed, nothing happens.

• Development builds no longer offer to update themselves. Attempting it
  used to end with "the signature does not match" — literally true and
  entirely unhelpful.

If the application is silent after a macOS upgrade, check System Settings →
Privacy & Security → Accessibility. An upgrade sometimes clears the
permission, and the application has no way of being told.
