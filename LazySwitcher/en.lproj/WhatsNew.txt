• Short words are finally corrected along with their neighbours: «не», «еще»,
  «нет», «как», «так», «тут», «а», «это». «не это» used to come out as
  «yt это», and «а откуда» was not corrected at all.

  Such words cannot be acted on alone — "a", "no", "он" are too often real
  words. They are meant to travel with a neighbour, and that was what broke.

• Fixed: a correction would fail on the first press and work on the second.
  You could see the word being selected and immediately deselected.

  The cause was small and expensive: before replacing anything the app checks
  that it is about to change exactly what it means to. macOS capitalises the
  first word of a sentence after we write it — so «Привет» no longer matched
  «привет». One capital letter aborted the correction and, on top of that,
  switched off carrying neighbouring words for a minute.

  On your hunch: that check is what stops a password or somebody else's text
  being mangled, so it has to stay. But it no longer objects to letter case,
  and it no longer blocks anything.
