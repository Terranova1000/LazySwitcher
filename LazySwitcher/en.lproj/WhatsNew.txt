• The main one: while typing quickly, the app could damage words — «как дела»
  came out as «rкак ела» or «как lела». It was replacing text while you were
  still typing, so its keystrokes and yours got mixed together.

  It now makes sure you have stopped first. If you type without pauses, the
  correction waits for the next pause and fixes the whole phrase at once.

• A word begun before a layout switch and finished after it is no longer left
  broken in half. The app now remembers which layout every key was pressed in
  and puts such a word back together.

• Names and brands typed right after a correction come back as English:
  «привет google как дела» now happens by itself.

• Undo is fixed: double Shift right after a replacement brings back what you
  typed again.
